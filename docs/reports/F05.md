   - Designed `EmptyState` and `Skeleton` loading states.

3. **Analytics & Reports Redesign (`frontend/src/pages/ReportsPage.tsx`)**:
   - "Synthetic prototype data" banner clearly presented on the header.
   - Four operational analytics charts in a 2x2 grid:
     1. **Bureau-wise Distribution**: Stacked bar chart showing Open (Blue `#2a78d6`), Disposed (Aqua `#1baf7a`), and Overdue (Critical `#B42318`).
     2. **Ageing Cohorts**: Open grievance distribution across ageing windows (Orange `#eb6834`).
     3. **Monthly Velocity**: Velocity dual lines for Received (Blue `#2a78d6`) vs Disposed (Aqua `#1baf7a`).
     4. **Overlap Severity**: Distribution of detected overlaps by confidence level (Critical, Orange, Blue).
   - **"Show Table" Toggle**: Every individual chart features an interactive toggle button revealing a detailed, tabular data breakdown.
   - Date range filtering (`dateFrom`, `dateTo`) with Apply and Reset controls.
